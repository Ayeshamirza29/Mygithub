    Safety Guidance

    Key rules for avoiding dangerous drug combinations and using medicines safely.

    Watch for bleeding risks

    Some medications increase bleeding risk when combined. Check the interaction report before use.

    Avoid sedation overlap

    Combining sedatives and pain medicines can cause excessive drowsiness or breathing issues.

    Review age-sensitive warnings

    Older adults may need lower doses or alternative drugs to reduce side effects.

    Know what to avoid

    Our checker highlights medicines that should not be combined for your condition.

    Understand medicine benefits

    Each recommendation includes the main benefit so you can compare options confidently.

    Stay informed

    Follow guidance from the interaction report and consult your healthcare provider for final decisions.
